# WP-DM6: can Z6 vacua with the K.10 bias be obtained from explicit breaking operators? (pre-registration)

Follows WP-VAC (phase manifold is a circle; Bond gives only harmonic n=1). Question: which explicit-breaking operators of the model produce which harmonics of the phase potential, and does any single one reproduce the K.10 bias pattern DeltaC(s)={0,15,24,27,24,15} (ratios 5:8:9)?
Model: triples sector (20 states), H(theta; eta, j) = H0d + O_JS + (1/6)(e^{i theta} X + h.c.) + eta (X^j + (X^dagger)^j) with fixed phase (Delta M = 2j), eta=0.02, j=1..6. Also the Bond with delta=0.05 as before. theta grid: 96 points.
T1: for each j: ||X^j|| in the sector, harmonics a_n (n=1..6) of E0(theta), a_n/eta.
T2: decomposition of DeltaC(s) in cos(n pi s/3), n=0..3 (the harmonic weights a bias must have on the six phases theta_k = 2 pi k/6).
T3: shape test: for Bond and each j with X^j != 0, the vector E0(theta_k)-E0(theta_0), k=0..5, normalised by its maximum, against DeltaC(s)/27 = {0,.556,.889,1,.889,.556}; report the max absolute deviation (match iff <= 0.10).
Success (for 'a single operator gives the K.10 bias'): some operator with T3 deviation <= 0.10 and the sign of the bias positive (E0 higher at k != 0).
Anticipation (registered before running): (i) X^j produces harmonic j only (other harmonics < 1e-3 of it); (ii) X^j vanishes in the 3-psion sector for j >= 4 (the six-fold harmonic needs a Delta M = 12 operator that cannot be tested with 3 psions); (iii) no single operator matches the ratios 5:8:9 (Bond gives about 1:3:4); success criterion NOT met.
Limits: finite 3-psion model; theta is a parameter, not a dynamical field; eta is a free amplitude (we test shapes, not sizes).
