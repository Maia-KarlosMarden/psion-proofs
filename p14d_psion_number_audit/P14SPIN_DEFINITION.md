# WP-SPIN: minimal spin extension and baryon magnetic moments (pre-registration)

Purpose: obtain an observable that the ring model does not fix by construction (magnetic moments), with the fewest new assumptions.
Postulates (all frozen before running; none fitted):
 S1. Each psion carries spin 1/2 (eq. 1). A triple has spin space (C^2)^3 (psions are distinguishable: 8 labels, no symmetrisation).
 S2. Spin Hamiltonian H_s = J * sum over L-R pairs (i in L={0,1,2}, j in R={3,4,5}) of s_i . s_j, equal weights; the sign of J is not fixed: both signs are reported.
     (The ring term C already couples every L-R pair, so this is the spin analogue of C. App. J.6.3's statement that only |2>-|3> is a real bond is NOT used here; it would leave p, n undetermined.)
 S3. Magnetic moment operator mu = kappa * sum_i q_i * sigma_i^z with q = Q9 = (1,-5,-5,10,4,4)/9 (moment proportional to charge, one universal kappa, equal psion masses).
     Moment of a hadron = <mu> in the ground multiplet of H_s with maximal S_z (J=1/2 -> S_z=+1/2; J=3/2 -> S_z=+3/2). Spatial structure is ignored (spin factorises from the ring state).
Triples: p=(0,4,5), n=(0,1,5), Lambda=(1,2,3), Sigma0=(0,2,4), Sigma+ in {(1,3,5),(2,3,4)}. Triples with no L-R pair, Sigma-=(0,1,2) and X=(3,4,5), have H_s=0: spin undetermined, not tested.
Observables (kappa cancels): ratios to the proton: mu_n/mu_p = -0.685 (observed, -1.913/2.793), mu_Lambda/mu_p = -0.219 (-0.613/2.793), mu_Sigma+/mu_p = 0.880 (2.458/2.793).
Observed values are the PDG nuclear-magneton values quoted from memory; they will be checked against a source before the paper uses them.
Criterion: for a sign of J (declared, either) and either Sigma+ candidate: |ratio - observed| <= 0.20 for all three. 
Null: the 720 permutations of Q9 over the six labels (lenient: breaks hadron charges); fraction of permutations (with the same sign choices, best of J signs and Sigma+ candidate) meeting the criterion.
Free parameters: 0 (kappa cancels in ratios, J magnitude irrelevant). Observables: 3.
Anticipation (registered before running): FAILS. The best sign choice misses at least one ratio by more than 0.2; the null fraction is not negligible (>=0.02), so even a pass would carry little weight.
Look-elsewhere: this is a new family (~#33 in the series); only the choices listed (J sign, Sigma+ candidate) are tried.
