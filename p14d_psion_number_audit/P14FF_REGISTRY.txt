CONCLUSION: the static hexagon with point psions reproduces the slopes (radii) but not the shape of the elastic form factors beyond Q^2 ~ 0.2 GeV^2. The static ring is killed as a charge-DISTRIBUTION description. This does not refute the composite claim: delocalised (dynamic) distributions, extended psions or relativistic effects are not tested here.
